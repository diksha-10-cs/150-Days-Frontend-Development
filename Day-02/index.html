Place 1

Dal Lake is a famous and beautiful lake located in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, India. It is often called the "Jewel in the crown of Kashmir" because of its natural beauty and mountain views. The lake is well-known for its traditional wooden boats called shikaras and floating houseboats where tourists can stay. It also has unique floating gardens where local people grow fresh vegetables and lotus flowers. Beautiful Mughal gardens like Shalimar Bagh and Nishat Bagh sit right along the edge of the lake. In the peak of winter, the water gets so cold that parts of the lake sometimes freeze over.


Place 2

Shanti Stupa is a famous white-domed Buddhist monument located on a hilltop in Leh, Ladakh, India. It was built in 1991 by Japanese and Ladakhi Buddhists to promote world peace and celebrate 2,500 years of Buddhism. The 14th Dalai Lama placed sacred relics of Lord Buddha at the base of the stupa. Its walls feature a golden Buddha statue and colorful artwork showing important moments from Buddha's life. Visitors can reach the top either by driving up a road or by climbing around 500 steep steps. From the top, you can enjoy wide views of Leh city and the surrounding snow-covered mountains, especially at sunrise and sunset.


Place 3

Hawa Mahal is a famous historic palace located in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. It is known as the "Palace of Winds" because cool air easily blows through its many small windows. The palace was built using red and pink sandstone by Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh. Its front wall looks like a honeycomb and was shaped like the crown of Lord Krishna. Royal women used to sit behind the tiny carved windows to watch street festivals without being seen by the public. Today, it is one of the most popular tourist landmarks in the Pink City of Jaipur.
